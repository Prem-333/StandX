# Synthetic fixtures

Every record here must have `source: synthetic`, an `IS-MOCK-` identifier, and a visible MOCK/SYNTHETIC label wherever displayed. These fixtures provide no evidence about real standards or certification applicability. Null fields mean unknown; empty relationship lists are not evidence that no relationships exist.

Phase 9 adds `phase9-tender.pdf` and `phase9-tender.docx`: small author-created MOCK/SYNTHETIC tender upload fixtures referencing an existing Phase 2 synthetic identifier. They are not IS standard full text or BIS publications.
